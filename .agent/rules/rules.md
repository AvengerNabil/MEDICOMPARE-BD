# MEDICOMPARE BD — Engineering Rules & Medical Guidelines

## Core Principles
1. **Medical Safety Rule**: Informational and comparison platform only, never a prescribing service. Never claim clinical interchangeability purely based on matching generic names. Distinguish exact composition matches, dosage form/release mechanism differences, and strength differences.
2. **Data Integrity & Provenance**: Only use verified Bangladesh DGDA/DGHS references and verified manufacturer data. Never invent or hallucinate medicines, fake prices, or fake popularity metrics.
3. **Frontend Aesthetics & Quality**: High-fidelity, international HealthTech startup design standard (inspired by Stripe, Linear, Apple Health). Polished typography (Inter/Manrope + Hind Siliguri for Bangla), generous spacing, mobile-first responsive design, accessible contrast.
4. **Bilingual First**: Seamless English and Bangla toggle with appropriate typography and cultural nuance without machine-translation artefacts.
5. **Architectural Separation**: Clean monorepo separation between `frontend/`, `backend/`, and `data/` pipelines.
